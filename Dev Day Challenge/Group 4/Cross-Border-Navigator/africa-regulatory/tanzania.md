## 2. Central bank and financial regulation
Central bank: Bank of Tanzania. Fintech ventures should plan on central-bank licensing for payment and lending products, AML/CFT registration, and consumer-credit rules. Cross-border digital services are increasingly taxed or regulated by regional frameworks (WAEMU/ECOWAS/EAC harmonisation).

Data protection: Personal Data Protection Commission (PDPC; verify operational status) — confirm the current regulator and registration thresholds before launching consumer-facing services.

## 3. Energy sector
Regulator / authority: Energy and Water Utilities Regulatory Authority (EWURA). Verify licensing thresholds for generation, the existence of net-metering or open-access rules, tariff structure (energy charge, demand charge, time-of-use), and any interconnection regime before building energy assets.

## 4. Sector compliance steps
The six Sita Sector priorities map to the following first-line regulators and steps (generic framework; verify the exact authority names and current fees):

| Sector | Primary compliance steps |
| --- | --- |
| Fintech | Personal Data Protection Commission (PDPC; verify operational status) licensing for payments and lending; AML/CFT compliance; data protection registration (18%); consumer protection. |
| Healthtech | Ministry of Health approval for products and services; medicines/pharmacy board where applicable; data protection for sensitive health data; medical device registration where relevant. |
| Edtech | Ministry of Education content and curriculum accreditation; data protection with specific attention to minors' data; intellectual property and content licensing. |
| Agritech | Ministry of Agriculture and plant-health authority (phytosanitary); land use and lease diligence; export/customs compliance where cross-border. |
| Retail & ecommerce | National revenue authority (VAT and digital-service taxes); consumer-protection authority; data protection at consumer scale; standards bureau for product compliance. |
| Energy & manufacturing | Energy regulator: 30%; environmental impact assessment (EIA); standards bureau certification; land/lease and utility agreements; special economic zone / export-processing incentives where available. |

## 5. Tax planning
| Item | Value (verify with national revenue authority) |
| --- | --- |
| Standard corporate income tax | 30% |
| Standard VAT / sales tax | 18% |

Investment promotion agency: Tanzania Investment Centre (TIC). Incentives: Export processing zones; special economic zones; strategic investor status.

Tax-planning considerations: holding-structure choices (local subsidiary vs branch), withholding taxes on dividends/interest/royalties (commonly 5–15%, verify per treaty), transfer-pricing documentation, digital-service taxes where enacted, and export-processing/special economic zone regimes where available. All rates require verification against the current national finance act or equivalent.
