## 2. Central bank and financial regulation
Central bank: Bank of Zambia. Fintech ventures should plan on central-bank licensing for payment and lending products, AML/CFT registration, and consumer-credit rules. Cross-border digital services are increasingly taxed or regulated by regional frameworks (WAEMU/ECOWAS/EAC harmonisation).

Data protection: ZICTA (registrar under data protection law; verify) — confirm the current regulator and registration thresholds before launching consumer-facing services.

## 3. Energy sector
Regulator / authority: Energy Regulation Board (ERB). Verify licensing thresholds for generation, the existence of net-metering or open-access rules, tariff structure (energy charge, demand charge, time-of-use), and any interconnection regime before building energy assets.

## 5. Tax planning
| Item | Value (verify with national revenue authority) |
| --- | --- |
| Standard corporate income tax | 30% (35% mining) |
| Standard VAT / sales tax | 16% |

Investment promotion agency: Zambia Development Agency (ZDA). Incentives: Mining-linked incentives; energy-sector reforms.

Tax-planning considerations: holding-structure choices (local subsidiary vs branch), withholding taxes on dividends/interest/royalties (commonly 5–15%, verify per treaty), transfer-pricing documentation, digital-service taxes where enacted, and export-processing/special economic zone regimes where available. All rates require verification against the current national finance act or equivalent.
