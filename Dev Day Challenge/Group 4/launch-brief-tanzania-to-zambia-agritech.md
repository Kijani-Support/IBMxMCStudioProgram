## 5. Regional Blocs & Cross-Border Corridor
- **Tanzania blocs** *(tanzania.md §1)*: African Union; EAC; SADC
- **Zambia blocs** *(zambia.md §1)*: African Union; SADC; COMESA
- **Corridor**: EAC → Shared SADC · Target also in COMESA
- **Shared framework: SADC**: Both Tanzania and Zambia belong to SADC. Regional customs tariff preferences, sanitary/phytosanitary (SPS) harmonization, and cross-border agricultural corridor protocols under SADC are your primary legal lever—verify certificate-of-origin eligibility.
- **Leaving EAC jurisdiction**: Zambia is NOT a member of EAC. Any EAC regional permits, customs-union exemptions, or mutual-recognition licenses from Tanzania stop at the border.
- **Entering COMESA market**: Zambia participates in COMESA (which Tanzania does not). Establishing a local subsidiary in Zambia can unlock COMESA regional trade access, but requires meeting COMESA rules of origin.
