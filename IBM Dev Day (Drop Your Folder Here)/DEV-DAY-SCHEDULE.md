> **Updated:** 2026-11-27 · The build activity is now **1 hour 30 minutes**, and each group gets **15 minutes** to demo.

## Run of show

| Block | Duration | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Build activity | **1 h 30 min (90 min)** | Groups build against their sector checklist (`README-checklist.md`) and run the whitehacking tests (`README-vulnerability.md`) in their own folder |
| Demos | **15 min per group** | Live demo of the build plus the key findings from the vulnerability review |

## Suggested split for each 15-minute demo

| Minutes | Content |
|---|---|
| 0–8 | Live walkthrough of what was built |
| 8–12 | Top vulnerability findings and how they were fixed or would be fixed |
| 12–15 | Q&A and changeover to the next group |

## Rules for timekeeping

- The build activity ends sharply at 90 minutes. Push your work to your group folder before demos start.
- Demos are hard-stopped at 15 minutes. Load your demo before your slot so the changeover fits inside the 15 minutes.
